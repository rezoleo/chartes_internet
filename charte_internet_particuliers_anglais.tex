\noindent This code of conduct is accompanied by the Rules of Procedures of the association, which must be adhered to. These regulations are available at: \url{https://ri.rezoleo.fr/}.

\vspace{.5cm}

\begin{enumerate}

\item The member is responsible for their files and the integrity of their workspace.

\item The member is solely responsible for their activities and the content they access or produce on the network.

\item The member agrees to comply with applicable laws, particularly regarding intellectual property. They agree not to share software or multimedia content without the author's consent.

\item The connection provided to the member is \textit{strictly personal}. They agree not to share or transfer this connection to a third party under penalty of expulsion.

\item The member is solely responsible for the use of their devices. They will be held responsible for any use made of them, even without their consent.

\item The member agrees to notify Rézoléo of any hacking attempts or theft of their electronic devices and accounts.

\item The member agrees to respect the privacy of other network users.

\item The member agrees not to disrupt the proper functioning of the network.

\item The member understands that they may be prosecuted independently of Rézoléo for any offenses they commit, particularly in matters of insult, defamation, and counterfeiting.

\item The member understands that the Rézoléo association must, upon request by the judicial authority, provide all data in its possession that can identify them.

\item The member agrees to respect the Rules of Procedures of the association and understands that they may be deregistered and prosecuted if necessary.

\end{enumerate}
